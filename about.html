Two partners. One factory.

Silkstone Fashion is a knitwear manufacturer in Egypt, owned equally by Boldbridge Capital and Aletex Group. Discipline from Barcelona, depth from Egypt, under one roof.

Made to be checked.

Sixteen sewing lines today with room for thirty-three, on a floor that holds fabric inspection, computerised cutting, sewing, rotary and continuous printing, embroidery, metal detection and packing. Nothing in the chain leaves the building. Clients include H&M, DeFacto and LC Waikiki, and retailers in the United States, and the factory holds WRAP, Sedex, three ISO standards and four fibre certifications.

Who stands behind the floor.

Boldbridge Capital

A UK-headquartered group founded in 2005, operating in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates, across fibre optic cable and precast concrete manufacturing, infrastructure and engineering, smart city development and strategic investment. Led by Hany Naim, a Forbes Business Council member with twenty-five years building infrastructure across the Middle East and Africa.

boldbridge.co.uk

Aletex Group

A circular knitwear group based in Canet de Mar, Barcelona, founded in 2008, designing and producing knitted collections for global fashion brands from trend research to the finished product. Its clients include Zara, Bershka, Pull&Bear, Massimo Dutti, H&M, Urban Outfitters and Anthropologie. Aletex gives Silkstone its European buying discipline and its route into Spanish retail.

aletex.net

Visit the floor.

Silkstone is part of the Boldbridge group's manufacturing interests in Egypt and is run from the floor, not from an office. Buyers deal directly with the people who make their order.

Department leads for cutting, sewing, print and embroidery, quality and packing are introduced to buyers at the first visit, and every department can be seen working on the day.
