Questions buyers ask first.

What is your minimum order?

20,000 pieces per style. Trial orders start from 10,000 pieces.

What do you make?

Knitwear across every everyday category: T-shirts, long sleeves, polos, henleys, tank tops, bodysuits, sweatshirts, hoodies, quarter-zips, track jackets, joggers, shorts, leggings, T-shirt dresses, lounge sets, baby rompers, sweaters and cardigans, all made to your tech pack. Our standard constructions are on the products page.

Which order types do you take?

CM (cut and make), CMT (cut, make and trim) and FOB full package, where we source fabric and trims and deliver to port.

Which certificates do you hold?

WRAP, Sedex SMETA, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, GOTS, OCS, RCS and GRS. Copies and certificate numbers go to buyers on request.

Is it duty free?

Into the EU and the UK, yes, under Egypt's association agreements. Into the United States, the QIZ scheme gives duty-free access for garments that meet its qualifying content rules. The Egypt page has the detail.

Can we visit or audit the factory?

Yes. Buyers are welcome on the floor, about an hour from Cairo International Airport. Choose "A factory visit" in the form above.
